Learning systems for science and decisions.

I study principled, efficient, and reliable machine learning for scientific discovery and intelligent decision-making.

I am a senior undergraduate at Sun Yat-sen University, majoring in Information and Computing Science, and I am currently a visiting student in the Department of Statistics at the University of Wisconsin-Madison.

Next chapter

I will join the PhD program in Industrial Engineering and Management Sciences (IEMS) at Northwestern University in Fall 2026.

Research Interests

My research aims to advance machine learning through principled, efficient, and reliable methods for scientific discovery and intelligent decision-making.

  1. Generative Modeling

    Developing statistically grounded generative models for both theoretical understanding and practical use.

  2. AI for Science

    Applying machine learning to challenging scientific simulation and modeling problems.

  3. LLM Agents

    Studying in-context learning and sequential decision-making under uncertainty to better understand agent behavior.
