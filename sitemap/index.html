Research Overview

My work focuses on generative modeling, AI for science, and LLM agents, with an emphasis on principled, efficient, and reliable machine learning methods.

I am currently a visiting student in the Department of Statistics at the University of Wisconsin-Madison and will join the Industrial Engineering and Management Sciences (IEMS) PhD program at Northwestern University in Fall 2026.
